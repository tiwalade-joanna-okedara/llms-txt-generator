AI crawlers: who can read your site?
Untick a crawler to block it. Blocking search and user-fetch bots can keep you out of AI answers.


Checklist
  1. Upload llms.txt to your site root, e.g. example.com/llms.txt.
  2. Check robots.txt doesn't block the search bots you want citing you.
  3. Make sure key pages render text without JavaScript.
  4. Keep the summary line factual. It's what a model reads first.
